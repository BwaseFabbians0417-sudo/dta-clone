Impact

32,000 Nigerians to Be Empowered with Free World-Class Training

The Federal Government of Nigeria, in partnership with Coursera, is offering 32,000 free learning licenses to empower citizens with in-demand digital and professional skills.

This initiative is part of the National Skilling Project, designed to bridge the skills gap, improve employability, and open new opportunities for Nigerians in today's competitive job market.
